# 🚀 Money Turbo – 1-Hour-a-Day Production SOP

**Goal:** Turn a daily hook into a fully-ready YouTube Short (9:16) that is advertiser-friendly and ready for monetization.

> **All tools used are 100% free with no credit card required.**

---

## ⏱️ Daily 1-Hour Timeline

### 1️⃣ Pick a Hook (0–5 min)
- Open https://trends.google.com/trends -> Filter to **"YouTube Search"**.
- Choose a phrase with high interest.
- Create a new file in `scripts/` named with today's date.

### 2️⃣ Script & Fact-Check (5–12 min)
- Ask ChatGPT:
  `Write a 30-second YouTube Shorts script about "<HOOK>". Include a bold claim in the first 2 seconds, 2 quick tips, and end with "Follow @YourBrand for daily tips".`
- Feed the script to Perplexity.ai:
  `Verify the facts in this script and provide a source link.`
- Add the source link at the bottom of your script file.

### 3️⃣ Generate Visuals (12–25 min)
- Open `prompts/image_prompt.txt` and copy your prompt template.
- Paste it into Bing Image Creator (https://www.bing.com/images/create).
- Download the best 9:16 image.

### 4️⃣ Animate Image (25–35 min)
- Open Runway Gen-2 (https://runwayml.com) or Pika Labs (https://pika.art).
- Upload your image and add prompt: `slow zoom in, slight camera pan, 5-second loop`.
- Download the resulting 5-second MP4 clip.

### 5️⃣ Voice-Over (35–45 min)
- Copy your script text into ElevenLabs (https://elevenlabs.io).
- Choose an energetic voice (e.g., "Bella").
- Download the generated MP3 file.

### 6️⃣ Edit & Captions in CapCut (45–55 min)
- Open CapCut (online or desktop).
- Create a project with **9:16 aspect ratio**.
- Drop in your animated clip and voice-over MP3.
- Click **Text** -> **Auto captions**. Style captions with high contrast.
- Add an end-screen text: *"Follow for daily tips!"*
- Export as 1080x1920 MP4 (no watermark).

### 7️⃣ Upload & Optimize (55–60 min)
- Go to YouTube Studio -> **Upload Video**.
- Title format: `<Hook> – YourBrand`
- Add 3 relevant tags.
- Post as Public!

---

## 📌 Pre-Publish Monetization Checklist
- [ ] No deceptive or false claims.
- [ ] Fact-checked with source link available.
- [ ] Visuals and voice-over are original AI generations you own.
- [ ] Video format is vertical 9:16 and under 60 seconds.
- [ ] First 2 seconds have an immediate hook text overlay.
